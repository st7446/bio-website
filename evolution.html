Protein Evolution & Bioinformatics

How proteins evolve over time and the computational tools used to study them. Reveals evolutionary relationships and predicts protein function.

Molecular Evolution

Sequence Evolution

Point mutations gradually change amino acid sequences. Synonymous mutations (silent) don't change protein. Non-synonymous mutations change amino acids. Conservative substitutions (similar amino acids) are tolerated; radical changes often deleterious.

Codon Bias

Different organisms prefer different codons for same amino acids. Reflects tRNA availability and GC content. Affects protein expression efficiency. Adaptation to tRNA pool improves translation speed and accuracy.

Gene Duplication & Divergence

Duplicate genes evolve different functions (neofunctionalization). One retains original function, other evolves new (subfunctionalization). Source of protein family diversity. ~50% of genes in humans arose from duplication.

Positive Selection

Non-synonymous substitutions preferred over synonymous (Ka/Ks > 1). Indicates functional advantage. Common in immune system proteins, reproductive proteins. Drives adaptive evolution in response to environmental pressures.

Purifying Selection

Removes deleterious mutations. Synonymous substitutions more common than non-synonymous (Ka/Ks < 1). Maintains critical protein functions. Most prevalent form of selection for essential proteins.

Horizontal Gene Transfer

Genes transferred between distant species. Common in bacteria. Accelerates evolution of new functions. Enables antibiotic resistance spread. Complicates evolutionary trees.

Bioinformatic Tools

Multiple Sequence Alignment (MSA)

Aligns multiple protein sequences to identify conserved regions. Progressive alignment methods (MUSCLE, Clustal). Reveals functional constraints. Generates position-specific scoring matrices. Higher conservation indicates functional importance.

Phylogenetic Analysis

Reconstructs evolutionary history (phylogeny) from sequences. Distance methods (UPGMA, neighbor-joining) or maximum likelihood. Creates evolutionary trees showing relationships. Molecular clock estimates divergence times.

Domain Analysis

Identifies conserved domains within proteins. Domains represent functional modules. Domain databases (Pfam, InterPro) catalog known domains. Predicts biological function from domain composition.

Hidden Markov Models (HMM)

Models probabilistic patterns in sequence alignments. HMMER tool for sensitive homology searches. Better than standard alignment for remote homologs. Captures sequence complexity and variation.

Structure Prediction from Sequence

Secondary structure prediction (PSIPRED, JPred). Fold recognition (threading). Target-template modeling (homology modeling). AI methods (AlphaFold) now enable accurate 3D structure prediction.

Genome Annotation

Identifies protein-coding genes in genomic sequences. BLAST searches against known proteins. Gene finding algorithms (GeneMark, Augustus). Functional annotation from homology. Critical for genome interpretation.

Applications of Protein Bioinformatics

Disease Gene Identification

Missense mutations found through exome sequencing. Bioinformatics predicts impact on protein function. PolyPhen, SIFT tools assess mutation severity. Identifies novel disease genes. Enables personalized medicine.

Drug Discovery

Identify promising drug targets through protein analysis. Structure-based drug design uses 3D models. Virtual screening tests candidate drugs in silico. Accelerates discovery and reduces costs. Computational tools de-risk development.

Pathogen Analysis

Characterize viral and bacterial proteins. Identify mutations conferring drug resistance (HIV, influenza). Predict epitopes for vaccine design. Monitor evolution during epidemics. Guides public health responses.

Metabolic Engineering

Design enzymes with improved catalytic properties. Directed evolution guided by structure. Heterologous expression optimization. Create novel enzymatic pathways. Applications in biofuel and chemical production.

Agricultural Genomics

Analyze crop protein biodiversity. Identify useful variants for breeding. Understand stress response proteins. CRISPR guides based on sequence conservation. Improve crop yield and resilience.

Synthetic Biology

Design artificial proteins with novel functions. Protein engineering guided by bioinformatics. De novo protein design using AI. Create biological circuits and sensors. Frontier of biotechnology.
